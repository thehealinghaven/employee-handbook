## Version 1.1 (ready for attorney review), September 3, 2026

- Twelve of fourteen open items resolved (01, 02, 04 through 12, 14) plus the bonus period. Two remain, both waiting on outside parties: 03 (DOPL supervisor approval) and 13 (new suite). See [Open items](/reference/open-items).
- **Time off rewritten.** "Paid time off" replaced by [Time off and the four-day week](/pay-and-time-off/time-off-and-the-four-day-week): no PTO bank, banked days and days on credit, unpaid full weeks. [Holidays](/pay-and-time-off/holidays) and [Sick time](/pay-and-time-off/sick-time) rewritten to match. [Final pay](/leaving/final-pay) updated: nothing to pay out.
- **Compensation mirrored to the hiring site.** [Production bonus](/pay-and-time-off/production-bonus) now states the published formula and the monthly bonus period. "Health stipend" renamed the [Benefits allowance](/pay-and-time-off/benefits-allowance). New [Benefits elections](/pay-and-time-off/benefits-elections) page with the cafeteria-plan, written-authorization, and salary-floor conditions. [CE stipend](/pay-and-time-off/continuing-education-stipend) mechanics set and Utah's 40-hour CE rule added.
- **New pages:** [Accommodations](/employment/accommodations) (ADA interactive process, religion, pregnancy under Utah Code § 34A-5-106 and the PWFA, PUMP Act nursing breaks); [Your rights at work](/conduct/your-rights-at-work) (NLRA Section 7, Utah off-duty expression, personal accounts, firearms in vehicles, pay notice); [Compliance map](/reference/compliance-map).
- **Compliance audit applied.** Every cite re-verified September 3, 2026. Corrections: pay notice at hire is Utah Code § 34-28-4; pregnancy accommodation is § 34A-5-106(1)(g) and (7); all DOPL rule cites moved to R156-60e (2026); LCSW hours track § 58-60-205(1) as amended 2025; workers' comp 7-day reporting is to the carrier under R612-200-1; exclusion screening duty sourced to the Utah Medicaid manual and the OIG bulletin; PLLC rules cited with the October 1, 2026 renumbering. [Clinical supervision](/employment/clinical-supervision) adds the January 1, 2027 supervisor deadline, 30-day DOPL filings, and the honest timeline note. [Scope of practice](/clinical-practice/scope-of-practice) adds client-notice and unlicensed-staff rules. [Drug- and alcohol-free workplace](/conduct/drug-and-alcohol-free-workplace) now states the practice does not test. [Records retention](/privacy-and-security/records-retention) adds the schedule and the 42 CFR Part 2 position. [Required postings](/reference/required-postings) re-checked against the Utah Labor Commission list.
- Attorney list in the README refreshed; one item closed (pay notice cite found), several added.

## How to update this manual

1. Edit the page in the repository.
2. Add a line here with the date, the page, and what changed.
3. If the change is material (it changes what someone is required to do), bump the version and notify the team. Material changes require re-acknowledgment.
4. If an open item is resolved, update its page, remove the "Not yet set" callout, and mark it resolved in [Open items](/reference/open-items).

The practice manager owns this manual and its changelog.
