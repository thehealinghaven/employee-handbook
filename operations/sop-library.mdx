This manual tells you the rules. The SOP library tells you the steps. They're designed to work together.

## The relationship

- **The manual** states policy: what's required, what's prohibited, why, and who owns it. It changes rarely.
- **An SOP** states procedure: exactly how to do a specific task in our systems. SOPs change often as the practice improves.
- Where they overlap, the manual states the principle and links to the SOP. If you find a conflict, tell the practice manager. The manual doesn't silently override an SOP, and an SOP doesn't silently override the manual; the conflict gets fixed.

## Where it lives

The SOP library is in the Operations shared drive, under Standard Operating Procedures. Start with `00-SOP-Index.md`, or open `Healing-Haven-SOP-Hub.html` for a browsable version.

## Reading vs. signing

You don't sign SOPs. You sign this manual's [acknowledgment](/acknowledgment), which covers the policies. SOPs are training references. Your systems and documentation training completion, recorded at onboarding, confirms you've been walked through the ones that apply to you.

## Contributing

If you find a procedure that's missing, wrong, or out of date, say so. If you own a process, you may be asked to write its SOP. The practice manager maintains the library and the hub.
